Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Sanad (“we”) provides an AI messaging assistant that sports businesses (“our clients”) use to reply to their customers on WhatsApp and Instagram, book visits, send payment links and reminders. This policy explains what data we handle and how.

Data we process

  • Your phone number or Instagram account and the messages, including voice notes, you send to a business that uses Sanad.
  • Details you choose to share in the chat, such as your name, goal, preferred branch and time.
  • Booking, attendance and payment status. We never see or store card details; payments are handled by the business’s own licensed payment provider.

How we use it

  • To reply to your messages, book and remind you of visits, and send you the payment links you ask for, on behalf of the business you contacted.
  • To give that business a report of its conversations and bookings.
  • To improve the quality of replies for that business.

We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

  • The business you contacted, which owns the conversation.
  • Service providers we rely on to run Sanad: Meta (WhatsApp and Instagram), our AI model provider, and our hosting provider, only as needed to provide the service.

Marketing messages

A business using Sanad will only send you offers or reminders if you have messaged it or agreed to hear from it. You can reply “stop” at any time to stop these messages.

How long we keep it

We keep conversation data only as long as the business needs it to serve you, and delete it when the business ends its service with us or on request.

Your rights

Under Saudi Arabia’s Personal Data Protection Law, you can ask to access, correct or delete your data. Contact the business you messaged, or contact us directly.
